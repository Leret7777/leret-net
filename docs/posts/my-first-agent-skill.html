I Published My First Agent Skill

I've just published my first agent skill. An agent skill is a set of instructions an AI assistant can pick up and follow, so it can do a specific job well, the same way every time.

Most of the skills being written for sales and marketing teams assume you have a stack: a CRM, an enrichment tool, a sequencer, a webhook firing when someone visits your website.

That isn't how large commercial teams work. They get a weekly export from a data team, open it, and turn it into a decision before the trading call.

What it does

So my first skill builds a weekly pipeline trading pack. Give it a pipeline export plus your orders and target numbers, and it tells you:

  • whether the week traded green
  • what moved since last week
  • when the pipeline is expected to land as orders

Then it writes the commentary.

Why I built it

The work isn't hard, it's just long. A trader or an analyst spends an afternoon cutting a big export down to the few movements worth reporting. I know, because that part came from my own time trading a cloud and IT services portfolio.

Variance analysis and discount benchmarking are next.

It's free and open source: commercial-gtm-agent-skills on GitHub.

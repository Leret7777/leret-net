About me

Analyst turned AI automation specialist. I turn commercial chaos into smart, friendly workflows that help sales and commercial teams win customers. I'm also a model with Brother Models, and I live in Manchester.

Photos: Brother Models

What I do

I work in commercial data and analytics, in the commercial strategy team of a large UK telecoms company. I sit with sales, commercial and trading teams, find what's slowing them down in their reporting, data and tools, and build the fix. That usually means dashboards, custom analysis, and more and more, automation and AI.

I work in Qlik, Python and SQL, pull data from Oracle and Google Cloud, and keep the work moving in Jira, Confluence and SharePoint.

Outside the day job

Organising GDG Cloud Manchester

I've joined the Google Developer Groups community in the North West as an organiser. Google Developer Groups (GDG) are free, local meet-ups for anyone interested in technology, run by volunteers. Our chapter, GDG Cloud Manchester, builds a community for tech people across the North.

My first event

The first event I helped organise was a Manchester meet-up on Wednesday 17 September 2025, 6 to 8pm, at Lloyds Banking Group's Westminster House. We had three speakers, including a Google Developer Expert:

  • Marton Kodok (Google Developer Expert): "Reducing BigQuery bills with BI Engine capacity orchestration"
  • Olorunfemi Davis: "The AI in Your Pocket: Building Offline Gemini Apps with the Browser"
  • Paul Gledhill: "Build high-performance apps for the edge with Wasm in Go"

AI, cloud, data or web: there was something for everyone, plus food, drinks and plenty of time to meet people.

Come along

Everyone is welcome. You can join the community and see what's coming up on the GDG Cloud Manchester page.
